Where Can 4 to 6 People Stay Together Near DDP, Seoul?

Short answer: Blue Plane Stay, an entire airplane-cabin themed flat near DDP for 4 to 6 guests, with two bedrooms (three queen beds), two bathrooms and a kitchen, about 3 minutes on foot from Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station, the station for DDP.

Why it works for a group of four to six

Four to six people usually means two rooms and a queue for the bathroom. Here bedroom 1 has two queen beds and bedroom 2 has one queen bed, and there are two full bathrooms, so nobody waits in the morning. The lounge seats everyone around one table, and the kitchen has an induction cooktop, a fridge, a microwave and a washer and dryer for longer stays.

How close is DDP

The flat is on Toegye-ro in Ssangnim-dong, Jung-gu. Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4 and 5) is about a 3-minute flat walk from the door via Exit 5, and DDP sits right at that station. Myeongdong is two subway stops away on Line 4 with no transfer, and the Airport Bus 6001 stop for the airport is about 1 minute from the building.

How four, five or six guests use the rooms

Four: two couples each get their own bedroom, one in bedroom 1 and one in bedroom 2, with a bathroom each. Five: a family of five can put the parents in bedroom 2 and three children or relatives in bedroom 1 with its two queen beds. Six: all three queen beds are used, two in bedroom 1 and one in bedroom 2.

Good to know

The flat is newly renovated, on the 3rd floor with no elevator. Check-in is from 16:00 with a smart lock, check-out by 11:00. It is a registered hostel business with Jung-gu Office, Seoul.

Questions

Does the flat really sleep six?

Yes. Bedroom 1 has two queen beds and bedroom 2 has one queen bed, three queen beds in total for up to six guests, with two bathrooms.

Is it a good fit for four or five people?

Yes. With two bedrooms and two bathrooms, two couples can each have their own bedroom and bathroom, and a family of five can use the two queen beds in bedroom 1 plus the queen bed in bedroom 2.

How far is Blue Plane Stay from DDP?

About a 3-minute flat walk to Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Exit 5), which is the subway station for Dongdaemun Design Plaza.
