Is There an Airplane-Themed Place to Stay in Seoul?

Short answer: Yes. Blue Plane Stay is an entire flat near DDP in Jung-gu, Seoul fitted out as an aircraft cabin: a curved fuselage ceiling with a light strip, overhead bins, porthole windows and mint jet-style bench seats around a table, with two real bedrooms, two bathrooms and a kitchen for up to 6 guests.

What the cabin looks like

The lounge is the cabin: curved ceiling panels shaped like a fuselage, overhead bins, a strip of indirect light and mint bench seats built like airline seats around an oval table with a wall-mounted TV. Porthole-shaped windows glow along the aisle and the sliding doors. The bedrooms and bathrooms are ordinary, quiet rooms with queen beds, rain showers and a backlit mirror.

Who it suits

Families with children, friends travelling as a group and anyone who wants a stay that is a story in itself. Because it is the entire flat, nobody else is on board. The flat is a registered hostel business with Jung-gu Office, Seoul.

Where it is

Toegye-ro, Ssangnim-dong, Jung-gu, Seoul. About 3 minutes on foot from Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Exit 5), the station for Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and about 1 minute from the Airport Bus 6001 stop for the airport.

Questions

Is the whole flat airplane-themed?

The lounge and aisle are built like an aircraft cabin (curved ceiling, overhead bins, porthole windows, jet-style seats). The two bedrooms and two bathrooms are regular quiet rooms.

Is it a real plane?

No. It is a flat on the 3rd floor of a newly built building in Jung-gu, Seoul, with an interior designed to look like an aircraft cabin.
