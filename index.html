What it is

A private flat fitted out as an aircraft cabin

In one line: Blue Plane Stay is an entire flat for families and groups of up to 6 in Jung-gu, Seoul, fitted out like an airplane cabin, with 2 bedrooms, 3 queen beds and 2 bathrooms, a 3-minute walk from Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Exit 5) and 1 minute from the Airport Bus 6001 stop for the airport. Walking times are measured from the building entrance.

Blue Plane Stay is a newly renovated entire rental unit in Jung-gu, Seoul. A curved fuselage ceiling with overhead bins, a glowing porthole window in the aisle, and mint airline-style bench seats around an oval table for the whole group. It is a single private home, not a shared hostel dormitory. The whole flat is yours, with no host on site.

Family or friends, your whole group stays together in one place, with a kitchen for breakfast and a washer and dryer, a short walk from Dongdaemun's night markets and two subway stops from Myeongdong on Line 4.

Getting here

From the airport to the door

  • Airport Bus 6001 from Incheon Airport: get off at the Baiton Hotel stop, about a 5-minute walk. Going back, board at the Toegye-ro 6-ga (Gwanghui-dong Community Center) stop, 1 minute from the door. No subway stairs with suitcases.
  • Gimpo Airport: Line 5 with no transfer to Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station, then Exit 5 and a 3-minute flat walk.
  • Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4 and 5), Exit 5: 3-minute flat walk, no hills.
  • Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) and the Dongdaemun night markets: on foot.
  • Myeongdong: 2 subway stops on Line 4, no transfer. Convenience store: about 10 seconds away.
  • Neighbourhood: Toegye-ro, Ssangnim-dong, Jung-gu, Seoul. The exact address is sent after booking.
Questions guests ask

FAQ

Where exactly is Blue Plane Stay?

In Jung-gu, central Seoul, on Toegye-ro near DDP. 3-minute flat walk from Exit 5 of Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, 5). Airport Bus 6001: the stop for the airport (Toegye-ro 6-ga, Gwanghui-dong Community Center) is 1 minute away and the arrival stop (Baiton Hotel) is a 5-minute walk. Myeongdong is two subway stops away on Line 4, no transfer. The exact address is sent after booking.

How many people can stay?

Up to 6. Two bedrooms with 3 queen beds in total, and 2 bathrooms so nobody queues in the morning.

Is there an elevator?

No. The flat is on the 3rd floor and there is no elevator. Ask in advance and the host can help with heavy luggage.

How do I check in?

Check-in from 4:00 PM, checkout by 11:00 AM. Self check-in with a smart door lock. The code is sent before arrival. Early luggage storage is available if you ask ahead.

How do I get there from Incheon Airport?

Take Airport Bus 6001 and get off at the Baiton Hotel stop, about a 5-minute walk to the flat. Going back to the airport, board at the Toegye-ro 6-ga (Gwanghui-dong Community Center) stop, 1 minute from the door. No subway stairs with suitcases.

Is it a licensed accommodation?

Yes. Blue Plane Stay is a registered hostel business with Jung-gu Office, Seoul. Registration no. 2026-000060, also shown on the Airbnb and Booking.com listings.

Where do I book?

On Airbnb, Booking.com (listed as Blue Plane Stay by DDP) or Trip.com. All three show live availability and prices.
