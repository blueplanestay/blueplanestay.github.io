What it is

A private flat fitted out as an aircraft cabin

In one line: Blue Plane Stay is an entire airplane-cabin themed flat for up to 6 guests in Jung-gu, Seoul, with 2 bedrooms, 3 queen beds and 2 bathrooms, a 3-minute walk from Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Exit 5) and 1 minute from the Airport Bus 6001 stop for the airport. Facts on this page were checked on 21 September 2026.

Blue Plane Stay is an entire rental unit on the 3rd floor of a newly built low-rise building in Jung-gu, Seoul. A curved fuselage ceiling with overhead bins, a glowing porthole window in the aisle, and mint airline-style bench seats around an oval table for the whole group. It is a single private home, not a shared hostel dormitory. The whole flat is yours, with no host on site.

It suits families and groups of friends of four to six who want one place to sleep, cook and gather, a short walk from Dongdaemun's night markets and two bus stops from Myeongdong.

Questions guests ask

FAQ

Where exactly is Blue Plane Stay?

In Jung-gu, central Seoul, on Toegye-ro near DDP. 3-minute flat walk from Exit 5 of Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, 5). Airport Bus 6001: the stop for the airport (Toegye-ro 6-ga, Gwanghui-dong Community Center) is 1 minute away and the arrival stop (Baiton Hotel) is a 5-minute walk. Two bus stops from Myeongdong. The exact address is sent after booking.

How many people can stay?

Up to 6. Two bedrooms with 3 queen beds in total, and 2 bathrooms so nobody queues in the morning.

Is there an elevator?

No. The flat is on the 3rd floor of a newly built low-rise building without an elevator. Ask in advance and the host can help with heavy luggage.

How do I check in?

Check-in from 4:00 PM, checkout by 11:00 AM. Self check-in with a smart door lock. The code is sent before arrival. Early luggage storage is available if you ask ahead.

How do I get there from Incheon Airport?

Take Airport Bus 6001 and get off at the Baiton Hotel stop, about a 5-minute walk to the flat. Going back to the airport, board at the Toegye-ro 6-ga (Gwanghui-dong Community Center) stop, 1 minute from the door. No subway stairs with suitcases.

Is it a licensed accommodation?

Yes. Blue Plane Stay is a registered hostel business with Jung-gu Office, Seoul. The registration is shown on the Airbnb listing.

Where do I book?
